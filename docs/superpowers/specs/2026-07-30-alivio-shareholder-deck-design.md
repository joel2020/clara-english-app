# Alivio Shareholder Business Case - Presentation Design

## Objective

Create a print-ready shareholder presentation that enables Eddie Murray to recommend Alivio as the lowest-risk, lowest-committed-cost route for converting the BoroDesk concept into a supported maintenance-intake platform for a 20,000-unit portfolio.

## Communication Job

By the end, shareholders should authorize a controlled Alivio pilot and 36-month founding-partner agreement because Alivio preserves the building employee's useful operational insight while adding governance, continuity, security, support, and materially lower portfolio-wide cost than a per-unit platform.

## Commercial Offer

- One-time installation and portfolio launch: $9,950.
- Monthly maintenance: $2,500.
- Initial term: 36 months.
- Coverage: up to 20,000 managed units within the agreed initial scope.
- Three-year committed cost: $99,950.
- Customer owns its data, branding, configurations, and customer-funded customizations.
- After the initial term, the customer receives a perpetual license to its installed deployment.
- Source escrow provides continuity protection.
- Alivio retains reusable platform code, frameworks, and general intellectual property.
- Major new modules, additional integrations, extraordinary telecom usage, taxes, replacement hardware, and exceptional on-site travel are excluded or separately approved.

## Savings Logic

The presentation will distinguish verified price mathematics from operating scenarios.

### Direct Software Cost Comparison

- Alivio: $39,950 in year one; $99,950 over three years.
- Published Yardi residential starting price of $1 per unit per month: $240,000 annually and $720,000 over three years at 20,000 units.
- $2 per unit per month market illustration: $480,000 annually and $1.44 million over three years.
- $3 per unit per month market illustration: $720,000 annually and $2.16 million over three years.
- Three-year Alivio savings versus those reference points: $620,050, $1,340,050, and $2,060,050 respectively.
- The deck must state that products are not necessarily like-for-like and custom enterprise quotes may differ.

### Implementation Cost Avoidance

The $9,950 installation includes discovery, configuration, a controlled building pilot, initial data import, one standard integration, staff training, documentation, and portfolio rollout configuration. The deck will show these as included cost categories, without inventing competitor implementation prices.

### Operating Efficiency Scenario

Use an explicitly labeled conservative illustration:

- 5,000 maintenance requests per month, based on 0.25 requests per unit.
- Six minutes of administrative intake and follow-up per request.
- $35 per hour fully loaded administrative labor.
- Current administrative handling: $17,500 per month.
- A 40% reduction in administrative handling: $7,000 monthly or $84,000 annually.
- Less Alivio maintenance: $54,000 potential annual net operating benefit after the first year.

The scenario is not a guaranteed saving. Actual request volume, handling time, labor cost, and automation rate must be measured during the pilot.

## Argument

1. The employee identified a real operational need and should be credited as a subject-matter expert.
2. A prototype is not yet a portfolio-grade operating asset.
3. A direct employee joint venture concentrates software, support, IP, security, and continuity risk in one individual.
4. Large competitors offer mature systems but generally require broad ecosystem adoption, per-unit or custom pricing, and vendor roadmap dependence.
5. Alivio offers a narrow, customized layer that can integrate with the existing system of record.
6. The founding-partner price caps committed cost while milestone acceptance and a controlled pilot limit execution risk.

## Visual Direction

- 16:9 landscape PowerPoint, designed to print clearly in color or grayscale.
- White canvas, black typography, pale gray structural fields, and one restrained Alivio blue accent.
- Large takeaway headlines, short body copy, and one primary message per slide.
- Native editable charts and tables.
- No decorative stock imagery is required.
- Use exact footnotes and speaker-note source blocks for external claims.

## Quality Gates

- Validate all arithmetic independently.
- Render and inspect every slide at full size.
- Run overflow and overlap checks.
- Convert to PDF and inspect every page.
- Do not claim custom-quote competitor pricing as published fact.
- Do not promise immediate ownership of Alivio's reusable platform.
- Present the labor scenario as an illustration pending pilot measurement.
